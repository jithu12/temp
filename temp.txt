Implementation of account lifecycle resource management in Dataviz to automatically handle stopping and deletion of resources based on account status events.

This change ensures that when an account is deactivated or deleted, all associated resources are properly handled to maintain system consistency and compliance.

* Stop all active resources when a deactivation event is received via Event Bus
* Delete all associated resources when a deletion event is received via Event Bus
* Retain specific internal resources required for legal, forensic, or regulatory purposes
* Introduce a garbage collection mechanism to periodically identify and clean up orphan/ghost resources
